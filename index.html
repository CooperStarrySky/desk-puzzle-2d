How to play

  1. Read the clues. Sixteen pieces lie strewn across the desk. Drag them around to spread out the pile and read what each one says.
  2. Some clues hide. Slides read on the microscope stage (each carries a letter, so you can tell them apart). Films read on the light box, wherever the film overlaps the glass.
  3. Lock in a group of four. Drag four pieces that belong together into a tray, then press Lock In. It locks with the group's name, colored by how tricky it was.
  4. Four mistakes. A wrong guess costs one of four tries, unlimited in Casual mode. "One away!" means three of the four belonged together.
  5. Scatter or reset. Scatter re-spreads the pile if it's gotten cluttered. Reset clears your progress and starts the puzzle over.

Keyboard: Tab to a piece, 1 to 4 sends it to a tray, 0 or Backspace returns it to the desk, V views a slide or film on its machine, arrow keys nudge it.
